# Brief 01-backend-setup

Owner: Backend. Phase: Setup.

Goal: be ready to propose a stack and publish a contract quickly once a prompt arrives.

Deliverables:
1. `docs/api/stack-playbook.md`: a decision guide covering the common website types we are likely to get (brochure or portfolio, small business with contact or booking, blog or content heavy, e-commerce lite, web app). For each: recommended stack and hosting, CMS options, form handling, costs, and what the client maintains. Check current versions and pricing on the web rather than from memory, and cite sources.
2. `docs/api/_templates/`: templates for `stack-proposal.md`, `contract.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `deploy.md`.
3. Verify the local toolchain (Node, npm, Python, git; anything else you would need such as a local database or serverless emulator) and list gaps in your report. Do not install large services yet.

Do not scaffold `server/` yet: the stack is chosen at Gate 1.
When done: commit your paths, write `.team/reports/01-backend-setup-backend.md`, send DONE to the PM, then wait.
