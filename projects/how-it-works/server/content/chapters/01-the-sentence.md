No style guide. No sitemap. No audience. Just a person who wanted a website and a thread that took them at their word.

The project manager's first move is unglamorous on purpose: it copies the sentence into a brief, word for word, and writes underneath it what it *thinks* success means, flagged as a guess. Everything the team does from here can be traced back to this file.

Then it lists what it doesn't know, and saves those questions for you.
