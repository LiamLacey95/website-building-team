# Brief 01-research-discover (project: how-it-works)

Owner: Research. Phase: 1 Discover.

Read `projects/how-it-works/BRIEF.md` first. The subject of the site is this team itself: read `README.md`, `BOOTSTRAP.md` and `.team/PLAYBOOK.md` so you can explain it accurately.

Deliverables in `projects/how-it-works/research/` (use `docs/research/_templates/`):
1. **Competitor and reference teardown (6 to 8):** sites that explain an AI or developer tool, process or pipeline in a memorable way. Include at least two explainer sites known for scroll-driven or interactive storytelling, two AI agent or dev-tool product sites, and one design-studio "our process" page. For each: what makes it work, what to avoid, screenshots in `refs/`.
2. **Personas:** 2 or 3 from the audiences in the brief, with what they need to see to try this and their likely objections (GPU cost, trust in AI design quality, setup effort, licences).
3. **Content inventory:** every concept the site must explain (roles, phases, gates, ComfyUI, worktree variants, screenshots, Impeccable, Review, setup), what real artefact from this project could illustrate each, and what copy we need to write.
4. **Reference board for Concept:** 12 to 20 visual references grouped into at least three candidate directions (for example: editorial or print, technical or blueprint, playful or toy-like), each with a one-line reason.
5. **Constraints:** accessibility of scroll-driven and animated storytelling (reduced motion, keyboard, screen readers), GitHub Pages limits, licence and attribution needs (Impeccable, models, fonts).
6. **SUMMARY.md:** one page, three insights that should shape the design, and your recommended answers to the brief's open questions with evidence.

Time budget: one working session, as in `docs/research/METHOD.md`.
When done: commit your paths, write `projects/how-it-works/reports/01-research-discover-research.md`, send DONE to the PM, then wait.
