# Brief 05-review-creativity-rubric (project: how-it-works)

Owner: Review. Phase: 3. Read `gates/G1-strategy/DECISION.md`: the human set the success measure as **"how creative, interesting and polished the final output is"**.

Your checklists measure correctness well, and creativity barely at all. Fix that before Gate 2.

Deliverables:
1. `docs/review/checklists/creativity.md`: a scored rubric for Gates 2, 3 and 4, usable for any project.
   - Score 1 to 10 on design, creativity and originality, content and storytelling, polish and craft (detail, transitions, edge states, consistency), and usability. Use Awwwards' public jury criteria as a reference point and cite them.
   - Concrete anchors for what a 4, a 7 and a 9 look like on each axis.
   - A "memorability" check: what one thing a visitor would describe to a friend.
   - A short list of AI-generic tells to penalise, using Impeccable's detector categories.
2. Wire it into G2, G3 and G4 (`docs/review/checklists/G2.md` to `G4.md`). Accessibility and the quality bar still apply; they don't stand in for creativity.
3. Calibrate: score two or three well-known award-winning interactive sites and one deliberately bland one with the rubric. Put the scores in your report, so the human can see the scale means something.

When done: commit with a pathspec, write `reports/05-review-creativity-rubric-review.md`, send DONE, then wait. Your next review is Gate 2.
