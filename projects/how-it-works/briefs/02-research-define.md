# Brief 02-research-define (project: how-it-works)

Owner: Research (with the PM). Phase: 2 Define. Ends at Gate 1.

Your Discover work was accepted as the basis for Define. Build on `research/SUMMARY.md`; its three insights are the design principles unless the human overrules them at Gate 1.

Deliverables in `projects/how-it-works/research/define/`:
1. **`positioning.md`:** one-sentence positioning, a value proposition per persona (Maya, Tomás, Sam), tone of voice (5 do/don't pairs), and three candidate hero lines that make the setup sentence the call to action.
2. **`sitemap.md`:** pages and their purpose. Start from your must-haves (story home with embedded case study, Set up, How this site was made, Credits and licences, `llms.txt` or markdown twin). For each page, list its sections in order, with the real artefact each section shows and whether that artefact exists now or only after a later gate (mark these "slot, filled at Phase 5").
3. **`journeys.md`:** one key journey per persona, step by step, from arrival to "copied the setup sentence" or "starred the repo", with where each objection gets answered.
4. **`story-outline.md`:** the home page as a narrative: the chapters following this site's own prompt through the double diamond and four gates, the loop-back at each gate, what the reduced-motion and no-JS versions show, and where the setup sentence appears.
5. **`metrics.md`:** what success looks like and how we could observe it without cookies (repo stars and clones, copy-button events if cookieless analytics is approved, Lighthouse and accessibility scores as published proof).
6. **`gate1-questions.md`:** every open question for the human in one list: the four from BRIEF with your recommendations, plus your three new ones, each with options and a recommended answer.

The stack proposal is Backend's (separate brief); point out anything in the outline that constrains it.
Time budget: half a working session.
When done: commit with a pathspec (`git commit -m "research: ..." -- projects/how-it-works/research`), write `projects/how-it-works/reports/02-research-define-research.md`, send DONE to the PM, then wait.
