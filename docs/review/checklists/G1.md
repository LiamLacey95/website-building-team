# G1 Strategy and scope: review checklist

Inputs (all in `projects/<slug>/`): `BRIEF.md`, `research/`, the Define-phase outputs (positioning, sitemap and IA, journeys, content model, metrics), and the stack proposal in `api/`.
Report: `projects/<slug>/review/<brief id>-g1.md`. Only BLOCKER and MAJOR stop a PASS.

## Research quality
- [ ] Every factual claim (market size, competitor feature, statistic) has a source link or citation, and the source was accessible in the last 12 months.
- [ ] At least 3 direct competitors or comparable sites are torn down, with screenshots or URLs, and with strengths and weaknesses for each.
- [ ] Reference sites are relevant to the audience and the brief, not just generally admired.
- [ ] Findings are separated from opinions or recommendations.

## Audience and personas
- [ ] Each persona cites the evidence it rests on (research finding, data, interview, brief). No persona is invented without evidence.
- [ ] Personas include goals, tasks, context (device, situation), and barriers, including access needs.
- [ ] Primary persona and primary task are named.

## Scope against the prompt
- [ ] Every goal and constraint in `BRIEF.md` (including the human's prompt verbatim) maps to something in the sitemap, journeys or metrics. List anything unmapped.
- [ ] Nothing out of scope has crept in without a note saying why.
- [ ] Open questions from intake are either answered or listed for the human.
- [ ] The client's revenue band is known or asked about, if FLUX Klein imagery may ship (its licence is free only under US$5M annual revenue).

## Sitemap, IA and journeys
- [ ] Each key journey can be walked through the sitemap step by step, start to goal, with no missing page.
- [ ] Navigation labels use the audience's words (check them against research), not internal jargon.
- [ ] Depth: primary tasks reachable within 3 clicks from home.
- [ ] Content model covers every page type in the sitemap; each field has an owner and a source (real copy, CMS, API).
- [ ] Legal and policy pages are listed: privacy, cookies, accessibility statement, terms, and anything sector-specific.

## Success metrics
- [ ] Metrics are measurable, tied to goals, with a baseline or target and a stated way to measure them (analytics event, form submit).

## Stack proposal
- [ ] Stack fits the need: static content uses static or SSR output; there is no SPA framework where a content site will do.
- [ ] Hosting, CMS or data layer, forms handling, and analytics are named, with cost and licence.
- [ ] The stack can meet the playbook budget (LCP < 2.5 s mobile, JS < 150 KB gzipped on content pages).
- [ ] Front-end and Backend agree on the contract boundary (`projects/<slug>/api/` has it or plans it).
- [ ] Accessibility, privacy (consent, GDPR or equivalent) and security considerations are noted.
